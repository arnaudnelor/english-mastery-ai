# User Guide

## First launch
Enter your name, current and target level, daily study time and preferred accent (Canadian, American or British). Tick *French explanations* if you want 🇫🇷 notes alongside English ones.

## Dashboard
Your estimated CEFR level, streak, study time, vocabulary progress, skill bars, your most frequent mistakes and **today's plan**. The plan gives most of the time to speaking and listening and adds more time for any skill scoring under 70%. Click an item to start.

## AI Conversation
1. Pick one of 14 modes (Free, Business, Job Interview, IELTS…), a teacher personality and a correction style.
2. Click **Start conversation**. The professor speaks first.
3. Click the **microphone**, speak, click again to send. Or type.
4. Under each of your turns you'll see: words per minute, long pauses and fillers; the corrected sentence; each issue tagged *grammar / vocabulary / naturalness / pronunciation / fluency* with an explanation; a more advanced alternative; and a prompt to repeat the correct version (🔊 *hear it slowly*).
5. Click **End & get feedback** for a session report.

Correction styles: **Immediate** (the teacher rephrases your error inside the reply, like a real tutor), **After each response** (a feedback card per turn), **End of conversation** (no interruptions; everything at the end), **Strict** (exam mode, every turn scored).

IELTS/TOEFL band numbers are **practice estimates**, not official scores.

### Hands-free mode
In a conversation, click **Turn on hands-free conversation**. Then just talk: when you pause for about a second, your turn is sent automatically. If you start speaking while the professor is talking, the professor stops and listens. Use headphones for the best results.

## Grammar Academy
20 topics (B2–C2) written for French speakers. Read, listen to the examples, then do the exercises: multiple choice, gap-fill, sentence correction, translation. Wrong answers feed your mistake tracker. Works offline.

## Vocabulary
**Review** shows due flashcards: say the word, flip, then grade Forgot / Hard / Good / Easy. Forgotten words return in the same session. **Word bank** lets you search and add words. A word becomes *mastered* once its review interval passes 21 days.

## Writing
Choose a type (email, essay, IELTS Task 2, report, cover letter, minutes), write, click **Evaluate**. You get an improved version beside yours, scores, line-by-line corrections, repeated words and upgraded expressions.

## Reading
Eight original texts from B2 to C2 (news, financial report, research, short story, opinion, essay, academic extract). A timer starts when you open a text. Gold-underlined words have offline definitions, and you can double-click any other word for an AI explanation in context. Click **I've finished reading** to see your speed, then answer main-idea, detail, inference, vocabulary and critical-thinking questions. Finally, write a summary to get AI feedback, including any phrases you copied instead of paraphrasing.

## Listening Laboratory
Six original recordings: conversation, meeting, lecture, interview, news and podcast, in Canadian, American and British accents. With an OpenAI key, each speaker has a natural voice, and the audio is cached so it replays offline afterwards. Without a key, Windows voices are used. Keep the transcript **hidden** first, answer the questions, then reveal it and click ↻ to replay any line. **Dictation** shows missed and misheard words; **Fill the gaps** targets idioms; **Expressions** lists the key phrases.

## Pronunciation Lab
- **Shadowing & read-aloud:** listen to the model (at 0.7–1× speed), record yourself, play both back, then **Check clarity with AI** to see which words a speech recogniser could not understand. You can paste any sentence.
- **Sounds (IPA):** the 22 key English sounds, with tips aimed at French speakers.
- **Minimal pairs:** the app says one word and you choose which you heard (ship/sheep, think/sink, hire/ire, work/walk…). Ten rounds per set, scored.
- **Word stress:** stress patterns and word families that shift stress (ECONomy vs ecoNOMic).

The clarity score measures intelligibility to a speech recogniser. It is a good guide, but it is not a phonetician's assessment.

## Video Academy
- **Animated scenes:** six original scenes with animated speakers and subtitles, linked to their listening exercises.
- **My video library:** add a video file you own (.mp4/.webm) or a YouTube link, plus subtitles (.srt/.vtt) or a pasted transcript. Click any sentence in the transcript to replay it, tick *Pause after each replayed sentence* for shadowing, and change the speed. **Create study pack** generates vocabulary, idioms, grammar from the video, comprehension questions, pronunciation tips and a summary task (needs an AI key).

## IELTS & TOEFL
A full IELTS Academic practice test: Listening (30 min, each recording plays once), Reading (60 min), Writing Task 1 + 2 (60 min), and Speaking Parts 1–3 recorded. Timers auto-submit. You get a band per skill, an overall band and your priority area. These are **practice estimates**, not official scores. You can skip Writing or Speaking if you have no API key.

### TOEFL iBT and Cambridge papers
Use the tabs on the IELTS & TOEFL page:
- **TOEFL iBT (2026 format):** Complete the Words, Read in Daily Life, Academic Passage; Choose a Response, Conversation, Announcement, Academic Talk; Build a Sentence, Write an Email, Academic Discussion; Listen and Repeat, Take an Interview. Scores are estimated on the 1–6 band scale.
- **Cambridge C1 Advanced and C2 Proficiency:** multiple-choice cloze, open cloze, word formation, key word transformations, reading, essay, listening and speaking, with a Cambridge English Scale estimate and grade.

## Business English
Seven specialisations (corporate finance & investment, banking & risk, economics, AI & data, project management & leadership, academic research, careers & networking). Each links straight to its vocabulary, role-plays (MBA case, investment committee, analyst meeting, PhD interview, networking…), readings, recordings and writing tasks.

## Tests & Assessments
- **Placement test** (about 40 min): grammar and vocabulary, reading, listening, writing, speaking. It sets your level and learning path. Retake it monthly.
- **Weekly check** (12 questions): built from your recurring mistakes and your flashcard words.
- **Monthly mock exam:** the IELTS test above.

## My Learning Path
A week-by-week route from your level to your target. Topics behind your recurring mistakes are marked and come first, and each week ends with a speaking role-play. Bookmarks (☆ on lessons, texts, recordings and videos) are listed on the right.

## Progress Analytics
Study time per week, mistakes per week and by type, skill trends, vocabulary status, mock exam history, and your full learning history.

## Search and reminders
Use the search box at the top left to search across all lessons, words (including French translations), texts, recordings, conversation modes and your videos. Set a daily reminder time in Settings for a Windows notification, or run `install-reminder.bat` to open the app automatically every day at that time.

## Settings
Start with **Test my setup**: it makes one real request to each service (AI model, correction engine, natural voice, speech recognition, Azure) and tests your microphone, then shows ✅/❌ with the reason for any failure.

API keys (stored in Windows Credential Manager), AI model, learning preferences, light/dark mode, **Back up progress**, and a table showing what works offline, what needs a key, and what's planned.
